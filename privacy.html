Ressa is a mirror, and a mirror's business is nobody else's. The app is designed so that everything the camera sees stays on your iPhone: no video is recorded, no photo is saved, and no camera image is ever uploaded. This policy explains the few places where information is involved at all, and the rights you have. It applies worldwide and is written to align with commonly applicable frameworks such as the EU/UK GDPR, the Swiss FADP, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and other applicable data-protection laws — whichever gives you the greater protection applies. Ressa 是一面镜子，而镜子里的景象与他人无关。应用的设计原则是：摄像头看到的一切都留在你的 iPhone 上——不录制视频、不保存照片、从不上传任何相机画面。本政策说明少数几处确实涉及信息的场景，以及你享有的权利。本政策在全球适用，并参照 EU/UK GDPR、瑞士 FADP、美国加州 CCPA/CPRA 等常见数据保护框架撰写——对你更有利的标准优先适用。

1. Introduction1. 引言

Ressa is a mobile application that turns your iPhone's front camera and screen into a personal makeup mirror ("the App"), published by its independent developer, Chua ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy describes what information the App and this website process, why, and what choices you have.

The short version: the camera feed is processed on your device only and never leaves it; the only setting we keep is your light preference, stored locally; and the only external communication the App can make is with Apple to handle an optional purchase.

Ressa 是一款把 iPhone 前置摄像头与屏幕变成个人化妆镜的移动应用（下称「本应用」），由独立开发者 Chua（下称「我们」）发布。本隐私政策说明本应用及本网站处理哪些信息、为何处理，以及你可以做出哪些选择。

简而言之：摄像头画面仅在你的设备上处理，从不离开设备；我们唯一保留的设置是你的灯光偏好，且存储在本地；本应用唯一可能的外部通信，是为处理可选购买而与 Apple 进行的通信。

2. Scope2. 适用范围

This policy covers the Ressa iOS application and the product website you are reading (ressa product pages hosted on GitHub Pages). It does not cover services operated by Apple — the App Store, TestFlight, In-App Purchases and your Apple ID are governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

本政策覆盖 Ressa 的 iOS 应用，以及你正在浏览的产品网站（托管于 GitHub Pages 的 Ressa 产品页）。它不覆盖由 Apple 运营的服务——App Store、TestFlight、内购与你的 Apple ID 均受 Apple 自身条款与隐私政策约束。

3. Camera use3. 摄像头的使用

Ressa uses the front camera for exactly one purpose: to show you a live mirror. Specifically:

  • The camera runs only while the mirror is on screen and active — never in the background.
  • The live preview is processed on your device and displayed on your screen. It is not recorded, not saved, and not transmitted anywhere.
  • No face detection, face analysis, skin analysis or any other automated interpretation of your image is performed.
  • No beauty processing is applied — but also no analysis: we don't look at your face, we only show it to you.

Camera permission is requested once on first launch. You can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings; the App will simply have nothing to show.

Ressa 使用前置摄像头只为一个目的：向你呈现实时镜面。具体而言：

  • 摄像头仅在镜面位于屏幕且处于使用状态时运行——绝不在后台运行。
  • 实时画面在你的设备上处理并显示在屏幕上。不录制、不保存、不向任何地方传输。
  • 不进行人脸检测、人脸分析、肤质分析或任何其他对画面的自动解读。
  • 不施加美颜处理——但也同样不做分析：我们不看你的脸，只把你的脸展示给你。

摄像头权限仅在首次启动时请求一次。你可以随时在 iOS 设置中撤销它；届时应用只是没有画面可显示而已。

4. Freeze frames4. 定格画面

The optional Freeze feature pauses one frame of the live mirror so you can inspect it. A frozen frame exists only in your device's memory: it is never written to storage, never added to your photo library, and is discarded the moment you resume the mirror, open the purchase page, or send the App to the background.

可选的「定格」功能会暂停实时镜面的一帧画面供你细看。定格画面只存在于设备的内存中：它不会被写入存储、不会进入你的相册，并在你恢复镜面、打开购买页面或将应用切到后台的那一刻即被丢弃。

5. What we store5. 我们存储的内容

Exactly one kind of app data is persisted: your light preferences (brightness level and light color), saved in your device's local app storage so the mirror comes back as you left it. This stays on your device, is never synced to a server of ours (we don't have one), and is removed when you delete the App.

Ressa does not require an account and does not offer one. There is no Ressa server holding user data.

被持久化的应用数据只有一类：你的灯光偏好（亮度与光色），保存在设备的本地应用存储中，让镜面每次都回到你离开时的样子。它留在你的设备上，从不同步到我们的服务器（我们没有服务器），删除应用时即被移除。

Ressa 不需要账户，也不提供账户。不存在持有用户数据的 Ressa 服务器。

6. Permissions6. 系统权限

Ressa requests only the camera permission. It does not request — and has no code to use — access to the microphone, photo library, contacts, location or any other system resource.

Ressa 仅请求摄像头权限。它不请求——也没有任何代码可以使用——麦克风、相册、通讯录、定位或任何其他系统资源。

7. Purchases7. 购买

The optional Ressa Pro upgrade is an In-App Purchase — a monthly subscription, a six-month subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase — processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details, your Apple ID credentials, or your card information. Apple provides us with no personally identifying purchase data. Purchase restoration on a new device is likewise handled between you and Apple.

可选的 Ressa Pro 升级是一项应用内购买——可按月订阅、按半年订阅，或一次性终身买断——完全由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 处理。我们绝不会看到你的支付信息、Apple ID 凭据或银行卡信息。Apple 不会向我们提供任何可识别个人身份的购买数据。在新设备上恢复购买同样由你与 Apple 之间完成。

8. Third parties8. 第三方

The App contains no third-party packages, no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no trackers. The only external communication is with Apple's StoreKit servers for product information, payments and purchase verification. Apple's handling of that exchange is described in Apple's privacy policy.

本应用不包含第三方软件包、广告、统计 SDK 或追踪器。唯一的外部通信是与 Apple StoreKit 服务器之间就商品信息、支付与购买验证进行的通信。该过程由 Apple 如何处理，请参阅 Apple 的隐私政策。

9. Your rights9. 你的权利

Depending on where you live, you may have rights such as access to, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, and the right to object to processing or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because Ressa keeps no data about you beyond a local light preference on your own device, exercising these rights is usually as simple as deleting the App. If you would like our help anyway, contact us using the details below.

视你所在的司法辖区而定，你可能享有诸如访问、更正、删除、限制或转移个人数据的权利，以及反对处理或向监管机构投诉的权利。由于 Ressa 除了你自己设备上的本地灯光偏好之外不保留任何关于你的数据，行使这些权利通常只需删除应用即可。即便如此，如果你希望获得我们的协助，请通过下方方式联系我们。

10. Children10. 儿童

Ressa is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Since the App collects no personal data and requires no account, no such data of children is collected knowingly.

Ressa 是一款通用工具，并非面向 13 岁以下（或你所在司法辖区的同等最低年龄）儿童设计。由于本应用不收集个人数据、不需要账户，因此不会有意收集儿童的此类数据。

11. Security11. 安全

The best-protected data is data that never exists. Ressa's architecture keeps your images on the device and out of any network, which removes the most common breach surfaces (servers, uploads, third-party processors). The App is distributed through the App Store, and updates are delivered through it as well.

防护最好的数据，是从不存在过的数据。Ressa 的架构让画面留在设备上、不进入任何网络，从而消除了最常见的攻击面（服务器、上传、第三方处理者）。应用通过 App Store 分发，更新亦通过 App Store 送达。

12. This website12. 关于本网站

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and includes no third-party tracking. The hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) for operational purposes, as is customary for any web server; we do not use them to profile you. Google Fonts may be requested from Google's CDN when loading the page; see Google's privacy policy for details on that request.

本网站是托管于 GitHub Pages 的静态站点。它不设置 Cookie、不运行统计、不包含第三方追踪。托管服务商会按任何 Web 服务器的惯例保留标准服务器日志（如 IP 地址与请求时间）以作运维之用；我们不会利用它们对你进行画像。页面加载时可能向 Google CDN 请求 Google Fonts 字体；该请求的处理细节请参阅 Google 的隐私政策。

13. Changes to this policy13. 政策变更

If the App changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and adjust the effective date above. Changes are published here — we will not notify you individually, because we have no way to reach you and no list to reach you with.

若应用发生变化并影响到隐私，我们会更新本页面并调整上方的生效日期。变更会在本页面发布——我们不会单独通知你，因为我们没有联系你的途径，也不存在可以用来联系你的名单。

Questions about this policy or about the App: dev.chua@gmail.com.

关于本政策或本应用的疑问：dev.chua@gmail.com。
